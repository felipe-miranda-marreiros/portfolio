# Experience

## Scope

`prototype/pages/experience/index.html`, the prototype's Experience Page, composed only from existing components in
the Portfolio's Visual language. Visitor mode: Read. The final front end reimplements it.

## Job

Recruiters arrive from Home or a shared link and scan titles, companies and periods in seconds; engineers stay for the
depth: each role's summary, achievements and technologies. Per the Vision, Experience shows every role, each with
company, title, period, location and work model, summary, achievements and technologies, then an Education and
certifications Section, then the Footer. A recruiter traces the career from the most recent role back in one scroll;
contact and the public repository stay one click away in the Footer. Mobile and desktop have equal priority.

## Decided

On 2026-11-23 the Owner chose, while shaping, that the Page opens with its title and a one-line intro, both Content.
Title only, and a title with the CV download, were turned down.

On 2026-11-23 the Owner chose one Education and certifications Section holding two lists, Education then
Certifications, each under its own small heading. One mixed list tagged by kind, and two separate Sections, were turned
down.

On 2026-11-23 the Owner chose that a certification may link to its credential when the Owner adds one, and is plain
text otherwise. Never linking was turned down.

## Structure

The Top bar; the Page header (the title in the Display role, the intro in the Lede role); the Role entries, most recent
first, on a shared hairline, with no heading of their own since the Page title names them; the Education and
certifications Section (a Section title in the Headline role, then the Education list and the Certifications list, each
most recent first); the Footer.

## Ranges

About 3 to 8 roles, each with 2 to 6 achievements and up to about 12 technologies. Education 1 to 3 items;
certifications 0 to 10, and with none the Certifications list and its heading are absent. Every text holds both
languages, long Portuguese wording included.

## Anti-goals

No timeline graphics, skill bars or company logos; no monospace eyebrow above a heading; nothing that repeats Home's
facts panel; no invented roles, employers or achievements: placeholders stand in, marked as such.

## Unresolved

The Page needs three components that don't exist yet, each in its own PR before this Page, with rendered options for
the Owner: a Page header (title and intro), a Section heading (a Section's Headline-sized title), and an Education and
certification entry, whose fields and credential link are decided there. The Role entry, Top bar and Footer exist.
