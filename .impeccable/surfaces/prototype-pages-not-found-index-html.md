# Not found

## Scope

`prototype/pages/not-found/index.html`, the prototype's not-found Page, composed only from existing components in the
Portfolio's Visual language. Visitor mode: Read. The final front end reimplements it.

## Job

A Visitor who followed a broken or mistyped address learns at once that the Page doesn't exist and gets back to the
Portfolio in one click. Per the Vision, its text is Content like every other Page's. It takes the Portfolio's Visual
language at every address, About me's included. Mobile and desktop have equal priority.

## Decided

On 2026-11-23 the Owner chose, from three rendered options, a title in words, a one-line intro and a Link back to
Home. A "404" numeral as the title, and the title and intro with no link, were turned down.

## Structure

The Top bar, with no Page marked current; the Page header (the title in the Display role, the intro in the Lede role);
a Link to Home; the Footer, at the foot of the viewport when the content is short.

## Ranges

A title of a few words and a one-line intro, in both languages, long Portuguese wording included.

## Anti-goals

No illustration, joke or search box; no status-code numeral as the title; no About me styling.

## Unresolved

Which language the Page answers in when the address names none is settled with the Vision's open decision on each
language's address. The intro is a placeholder marked as such until the Owner supplies it.
