# Design System: Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Bold Facts, Quiet Person"**

The Portfolio's Visual language is a dark, confident engineering page where bold, tightly tracked type and hairline
panels carry the authority and the facts lead. The Owner stays in the background: a small, plain photo beside the name,
never a portrait as the centrepiece.

The system is monochrome bone and ink. Dark is the default, always: a near-black ground carries warm bone ink. The
complete light theme puts the same ink on bone paper and appears only when the visitor picks it in the dark and light
switch, never from the system preference. Every colour token resolves both themes through `light-dark()`, so the active
colour scheme picks one. Secondary text, metadata, hairlines and tints are the ink itself at fixed alpha steps; there is
no hued accent anywhere. The primary action is the ink, filled; focus is an ink ring. The only hue in the system is
error.

About me has its own, entirely distinct Visual language. It restyles the shared navigation, language toggle and Footer
through tokens alone; their place and behavior never change.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dark by default, always; light a complete equal the visitor switches to; both WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Monochrome: bone ink on near-black, ink on bone paper, secondary ink and hairlines by alpha steps of the ink.
- No hued accent: the primary action is filled ink, every other button is outlined in a hairline, row links are
  unboxed text, focus is an ink ring.
- Bold display grotesk with tight tracking; monospace only for labels, tags, chips and numerals.
- Hairline-bordered panel grids whose cells share borders, instead of cards and shadows.
- Facts over the person: a hairline facts panel leads Home, the photo stays small.

### About me

**Creative North Star: "Loud Bands, Quiet Person"**

About me's Visual language sets the Owner's interests, recommendations and studies like a gig poster: flat colour
bands, one per Section, a condensed uppercase display that runs the band's width, and lists set as a poster's billing.
It is loud and warm where the Portfolio is quiet, and it never looks like a social profile, a gamer costume, the
Portfolio recoloured, or anything corporate or cold.

It loads over the Portfolio's tokens and redefines the same names, so the shared components take most of its look
through tokens; the differences that have no token yet are listed under Components. Spacing and motion stay the
Portfolio's. It has one theme and no photo. Each band sets every colour token itself, so a component inside a band
takes that band's colours. Illustration is optional: the language works without it, and the first band reserves a slot
for one.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Four flat bands, purple, sunset orange, lime and charcoal, rotating one per Section.
- One theme and no theme switch; no photo; the language toggle keeps its place.
- Big Shoulders Display, condensed and uppercase, sized to the band's width; Archivo for everything else.
- Lists as poster billing: the category in large display type in the band's two key colours, the item in heavy
  uppercase Archivo in the band's ink.
- One ink per band at full strength, no alpha steps; 3px ink rules and square corners.
- No shadows, no blur, no gradients, no neon; an error is a dashed ink rule and its message, never a hue.

## Colors

The Portfolio's Visual language is strictly monochrome: one ink and one ground per theme, every other colour is that ink
at a lower alpha or mixed into the ground, and error is the only hue.

### Primary
- **Bone** (night theme) and **Ink** (paper theme): the ink is the primary colour. It sets headings and body text,
  fills the primary action (with the ground as its text), draws the focus ring, and inverts for text selection. Accent
  and caret colour are the ink too.
- **Display Ink** (`--color-display`): the colour display type and headlines take. On the Portfolio it is the ink
  itself; the token exists so About me can set display type and headlines in a band colour through tokens alone.
- **Key** and **Key Alt** (`--color-key`, `--color-key-alt`): the ink itself on the Portfolio. Both sets name them, so
  About me can set billing categories and hover underlines in its band's two key colours.

### Neutral
- **Night** and **Paper**: the ground of the dark and light themes; also the text colour on a filled-ink action.
- **Night Raised** and **Paper Raised**: the surface for anything that floats above the page (menus, popovers), always
  paired with the float shadow.
- **Secondary Ink** (84% of the ink): supporting lines, ledes, body copy in panels, resting row links and inactive
  switch options.
- **Muted Ink** (64% of the ink): metadata, captions, notes and footer text. Still AA on the ground in both themes.
- **Hairline** (10% of the ink): panel-grid borders and the rules under the top bar and above the footer.
- **Hairline Hover** (26%): the brightened edge of a hovered or focused panel and of a hovered outlined control.
- **Hairline Control** (22%): the outline of secondary controls, the theme switch and swatch edges.
- **Hairline Input** (52% ink on paper, 44% bone on night): input borders, which identify the control and so meet 3:1
  against the ground.
- **Tint** (7%) and **Tint Hover** (4%): chip fills and the pressed theme option; the faint fill of a hovered control.
- **Night Wash** and **Paper Wash** (2% of the ink mixed into the ground): the hovered or focused panel cell. Opaque,
  because the cell covers the hairline drawn behind the grid.

### Error
- **Error on Paper** and **Error on Night**: error borders and messages only, never decoration.

### Named Rules
**The Ink Is The Action Rule.** There is no accent colour. The primary action is the ink filled, with the ground as its
text: one per view. Secondary buttons are outlined in Hairline Control; row links stay unboxed. Focus is an ink ring.
If a view needs colour to show what to do next, the hierarchy is wrong, not the palette.

**The Alpha Steps Rule.** Secondary, muted, hairline and tint colours are always the ink mixed toward transparent at
their fixed steps (84, 64, 26, 22, 10, 7, 4), and the Wash is the ink at 2% mixed into the ground; never new greys. The
exceptions are few and named: input borders, set per theme to hold 3:1 (52% ink on paper, 44% bone on night); the
raised surfaces (Night Raised, Paper Raised); and the float shadow, whose ring and shadow are the ink at 6% and 22% on
paper and bone at 30% over black at 60% on night, because no ink is darker than the night ground.

**The Only Hue Is Error Rule.** Error is the sole hued colour, and it appears only where something went wrong.

**The Two Equal Themes Rule.** Every colour token exists in both themes and passes AA in both; dark is designed first,
never light inverted.

### About me

The Overview's "the only hue in the system is error" and the four Named Rules above (Ink Is The Action, Alpha Steps,
Only Hue Is Error, Two Equal Themes) are the Portfolio's; on About me the One Band Per Section, One Ink, Cream Is Large
and No Error Hue Rules below replace them.

Four flat bands and five inks. Every band redefines the semantic colour tokens (ground, ink, display, key, key alt,
action, on action), so nothing on About me picks a palette colour directly.

**Bands** (the ground of a Section):
- **Purple** (`about-me-purple`): Lilac ink, Lime display, Lime and Blush keys, a Lime action with Purple text. The bar
  and the intro sit on it; the Footer, like any Section, takes the next band in the rotation.
- **Sunset Orange** (`about-me-sunset`): Charcoal ink, Cream display, Charcoal and Cream keys, a Charcoal action with
  Cream text.
- **Lime** (`about-me-lime`): Charcoal ink, Magenta display, Magenta and Purple keys, a Magenta action with Lime text.
- **Charcoal** (`about-me-charcoal`): Lilac ink, Peach display, Lime and Peach keys, a Peach action with Charcoal text.

**Inks** (only ever on a band):
- **Lilac** and **Charcoal**: the band's ink on the dark and the light bands: text, rules, input borders, the focus
  ring, selection and error.
- **Lime**, **Cream**, **Magenta** and **Peach**: display type and Section titles, and the hovered edge of a control
  (`--color-hairline-hover` is the display colour).
- **Blush**, Purple and Charcoal, with the display inks: the key colours of billing categories and of hover underlines.

#### Named Rules
**The One Band Per Section Rule.** Every Section owns one band, and the bands rotate purple, sunset, lime, charcoal. A
component never sets a palette colour; it reads the tokens of the band it sits in.

**The One Ink Rule.** Each band has one ink at full strength: secondary, muted, hairline, control and input colours are
the ink itself, and tints are transparent. On sunset any alpha step below full ink drops under 4.5:1. Three lines take
another colour: the lineup's block rules are in Key; a hovered edge (`--color-hairline-hover`) is the Display colour;
and hover and focus underlines on the language toggle, band links and lineup items are 3px in Key or Key Alt.

**The Cream Is Large Rule.** Cream holds only 3.3:1 on sunset, so on the sunset ground it sets large type only: display,
headlines and the billing keys. On the charcoal action fill it is the action's text, at 15:1.

**The No Error Hue Rule.** No single hue reads on all four bands, so error is the band's ink, drawn as a dashed rule
with its message, never a colour alone.

## Typography

**Display and Body Font:** Schibsted Grotesk (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui), self-hosted, variable 400 to 900
**Label/Mono Font:** Martian Mono (with ui-monospace), self-hosted, variable 100 to 800

**Character:** Heavy, tightly tracked headings that sound sure of themselves over calm, readable body text, with a
monospace that labels facts the way a codebase labels things: commands, tags, counts, dates.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, fluid up to 6rem, line-height 0.95, -0.04em): the Owner's name and Page titles, balanced wrapping.
- **Headline** (700, fluid up to 3rem, line-height 1.05, -0.03em): Section titles.
- **Title** (600, 1.25rem, line-height 1.3, -0.01em): role titles, panel titles and list rows.
- **Lede** (400, fluid 1.125rem to 1.25rem, line-height 1.4): the supporting line under a Page title, in secondary
  ink, measure 52ch.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, line-height 1.6): summaries and supporting lines in secondary ink, measure 68ch.
- **Small** (400, 0.875rem): captions, notes, metadata in muted ink; also the size of row links and controls, which
  take the control weight (600), kept apart from the title weight so each can change alone.
- **Label** (Martian Mono 500, 0.6875rem, 0.08em, uppercase, tabular numerals): stat labels and dates. Inside chips and
  code, the same mono size is set as-is, with no tracking or case change.

Headings hyphenate and, where the browser has no dictionary, break long words rather than overflow a narrow column: a
Portuguese title such as "Responsabilidades" at display size must fit a 320px screen. Paragraphs wrap with a pretty rag.

Display type and headlines take `--font-display` (the sans), `--case-display` (none) and `--color-display`; titles,
including the bar's name and panel titles, take `--font-display` and `--case-display`; labels take `--case-label`
(uppercase), the lede's measure is `--measure-lede` (52ch), and billing items take `--text-billing` (the title size). On
the Portfolio they restate the hierarchy above; both sets name them, so About me can restyle a shared component through
them.

### Named Rules
**The Mono Labels Rule.** Monospace sets labels, tags, chips and numerals, never sentences or headings, and never as an
eyebrow above a heading: the heading carries its own weight.

### About me

**Display Font:** Big Shoulders Display (with Arial Narrow, sans-serif-condensed), self-hosted, variable 100 to 900,
always uppercase (`--case-display: uppercase`)
**Body and Label Font:** Archivo (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui), self-hosted, variable 100 to 900. About me has no
monospace: `--font-mono` is Archivo.

**Character:** Poster lettering: a tall, condensed, black-weight display that fills the band, over a sturdy grotesk
that sets the billing heavy and uppercase and reads plainly at body size.

#### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, 24cqi from 3.5rem to 20rem, line-height 0.88, uppercase, Display colour): the Page title, balanced
  wrapping.
- **Headline** (900, fluid 2.25rem to 3.5rem, line-height 0.95, 0.01em, uppercase, Display colour): Section titles.
- **Title** (Big Shoulders 800, 1.375rem, line-height 1.1, 0.01em, uppercase): billing categories and item titles; the
  bar's name takes this size at 900.
- **Billing** (Archivo 800, fluid 1.5rem to 2rem, uppercase): the items of a lineup.
- **Lede** (400, fluid 1.125rem to 1.25rem, line-height 1.45): one line under the Page title, measure 52ch.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, line-height 1.6): stories and notes in the band's ink, measure 68ch.
- **Small** (0.875rem): controls and error messages, at the control weight (700).
- **Label** (Archivo 800, 0.8125rem, line-height 1.3, 0.06em): labels, the language toggle and band links, uppercase
  through `--case-label`; chips and code at the same size are set as-is, with no case change, because token names are
  case-sensitive.

#### Named Rules
**The Band-Wide Title Rule.** The Page title is sized from its container's width (24cqi, from 3.5rem to 20rem), so it
runs the band's width like a poster's lettering.

**The Billing Rule.** A list is a lineup: the category in the display face at title size, large type, in the band's two
key colours, alternating Key and Key Alt down the list; the item in heavy uppercase Archivo at billing size in the
band's ink; two 3px rules in Key above and below. On sunset the keys are charcoal and cream, so half the categories
share the ink and stand apart by face and size.

## Layout

A single content column (76rem max) with a fluid gutter (1.25rem to 3rem) and a shared left edge; the top bar and footer
span the full width but align their content to the same edge. One 4px-based spacing rhythm (0.25rem to 8rem), with more
space above a heading than below it: the Page opens with 4rem above the title on narrow screens and 6rem from 44rem.

Collections read as hairline panel grids: cells share one-pixel borders drawn by the grid's hairline showing through a
one-pixel gap, and each cell (1.5rem internal gap) ends in a row link pushed to its foot, so neighbouring plain row links
share a baseline. Cells pad 1.5rem on narrow screens and 2rem from 44rem. The grid takes three steps, always in source order:
one column on mobile; two columns from 44rem, where wide cells span both and compact ones pair up; three columns from
68rem, where wide cells span two and full-width rows close the grid across all three. Mobile and desktop have equal
priority: grids collapse to one column and splits stack, heading first, and nothing a desktop row shows is dropped on
mobile. A specimen that is a single row of samples shrinks its samples to stay on one row rather than orphan the last.

### About me

The Portfolio's column (76rem max), gutter and spacing rhythm, unchanged. Every band runs full-bleed and aligns its
content to the shared edge. Content bands pad 4rem above and below on narrow screens and 6rem from 44rem; the intro
follows them like every content band. The bar pads 1rem above and below, the Footer 2rem. From 44rem the intro splits
into the title column and the illustration slot (12rem to 18rem), aligned to their foot; on narrow screens the slot
stacks under the title. A lineup row stacks category over item, and from 36rem sets them in two columns (a 9rem
category column, 1rem gap) on a shared baseline. Swatches fill an auto-fit grid with a 13rem minimum.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth comes from hairlines and alpha tints, not shadows: a hovered or focused panel brightens its edge
to Hairline Hover over the Wash, a hovered control gains Tint Hover. Only a surface that floats above the page
(menu, popover) takes the raised surface colour and the float shadow. Nothing glows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Float** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px <ring>, 0 12px 32px -12px <shadow>`): floating surfaces only. On night the ring is
  bone at 30% over a black shadow at 60%, because a dark shadow vanishes on the near-black ground; on paper it is ink at
  6% over an ink shadow at 22%. Every part derives from the palette except the night shadow, which is black.

### Named Rules
**The Hairline Before Shadow Rule.** Reach for a hairline or a tint step first; a shadow is the last resort and only
for something floating above the page.

### About me

Flat and printed: nothing casts a shadow and nothing blurs. Structure is drawn with thick ink rules (3px) where the
Portfolio draws hairlines, and a change of band is the only change of depth. The float token becomes a solid 3px ring in
the band's ink with no shadow, and a floating surface takes the band's ground.

#### Named Rules
**The Printed Flat Rule.** Nothing on About me casts or blurs; separate with a 3px ink rule or a change of band.

## Shapes

Softly rounded, product-like corners: chips at 4px, buttons, inputs and controls at 8px, the photo at 10px, panel grids
at 12px, which round only their outer corners and clip their cells. Fully round (999px) is reserved for things that are
truly circular. One-pixel hairlines are the structural line; no thick borders, no decorative shapes. The Owner's photo
is small, in a softly rounded square with a hairline edge, plain, with no effect applied.

### About me

Square, like a printed poster: chips, controls, the photo radius and panels at 0; only truly round things stay round
(999px). Every line is a 3px rule in the band's ink, except the lineup's block rules, in Key; a hovered edge, in the
Display colour; and the hover and focus underlines of the language toggle, band links and lineup items, in Key or Key
Alt. The illustration slot is 3:1 on narrow screens and 4:5 from 44rem.

## Components

No component library exists yet; these are the primitives the prototype's token specimens establish, to be confirmed
by each component's own PR. Every component takes every visual value from tokens and holds no visible text.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px), Small text at the control weight (600), padding 0.5rem by 1rem.
- **Primary:** filled ink with the ground as text, no border. One per view.
- **Secondary:** transparent, ink text, outlined in Hairline Control; on hover the outline moves to Hairline Hover and
  the fill to Tint Hover.
- **Focus:** a 2px ink ring at 2px offset. **Disabled:** 0.4 opacity. Transitions run 150ms on the expo ease-out.

### Chips
- **Style:** Martian Mono at label size, Tint fill, Secondary Ink text, 4px corners, padding 2px by 0.5rem, no border.

### Panel grid cells
- **Corner Style:** the grid rounds its outer corners (12px); cells are square.
- **Background:** the ground; on hover or focus within, the Wash and an inset Hairline Hover edge (200ms).
- **Title:** the Title role (600).
- **Border:** shared one-pixel hairlines. **Internal Padding:** 1.5rem on narrow screens, 2rem from 44rem.
- **Row link:** Small text at the control weight in Secondary Ink with an arrow; on panel hover or focus it goes to full ink and the
  arrow nudges 2px. The first cell's row link is the filled-ink primary action.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Hairline Input border (1px), 8px corners, padding 0.5rem by 0.75rem, Small text.
- **Focus:** the shared 2px ink ring at 2px offset.
- **Error:** the border becomes 1px Error and a Small message in Error sits under the field. **Disabled:** 0.4 opacity.
- **Error line:** solid (`--error-line-style`); About me sets it to dashed.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** on a bottom hairline, the name left (800, title size, -0.03em), controls right.
- **Theme switch:** a two-option segmented control, Dark and Light, with Dark pressed until the visitor picks Light, in
  a Hairline Control outline (8px); options in Small text at the control weight, Secondary Ink, full ink on hover, Tint fill and full
  ink when pressed. Switching crossfades every token at once (240ms).

### About me

The shared components take About me's square corners, 3px rules and band colours through tokens. These differences
have no token yet, so the prototype sets them in About me's page CSS; each component's PR adds the token it needs:
- the chip's border and tracking: a 3px ink outline and 0.06em on About me, a Tint fill with no border on the Portfolio;
- the toggle's pressed state: an ink fill with ground text on About me, a Tint fill on the Portfolio;
- the toggle's and links' type role: Label on About me, Small at the control weight on the Portfolio;
- the link hover: an underline in Key, a 3px nudge and its own arrow on About me, a colour change and a 2px nudge on
  the Portfolio;
- the error message's weight: the control weight (700) on About me;
- the chip's weight and gap: 400 and `--space-1` on the Portfolio, `--weight-label` and `--space-2` on About me;
- the Footer: a hairline above it and 1.5rem block padding on the Portfolio, no top rule and 2rem block padding on About
  me.

- **Buttons:** the primary fills Action with On Action text, no border, at the control weight (700); the secondary is
  outlined in a 3px ink rule that moves to the Display colour on hover, with no fill. **Focus:** a 3px ink ring at 3px
  offset. **Disabled:** 0.4 opacity.
- **Inputs / Fields:** a 3px ink border, square. **Error:** the border becomes a dashed 3px ink rule
  (`--error-line-style: dashed`) with a Small message at the control weight, in ink.
- **Chips:** Archivo at label size, set as-is with no case change, square, outlined in a 3px ink rule on a transparent
  fill.
- **Navigation:** the bar sits on purple over a 3px ink rule, the name left in Big Shoulders 900 uppercase at title
  size, the language toggle right: a square, 3px-outlined EN and PT control in uppercase label type that underlines in
  the Key colour on hover and fills with the ink, ground as text, when pressed. There is no theme switch.
- **Band link:** label type with an arrow; on hover or focus it underlines 3px in the Key colour and the arrow nudges
  3px (150ms). Its action variant fills Action and does not underline.
- **Footer:** the next band in the rotation, like any Section; Small text, with a band link.

#### Lineup
The signature component, the billing row of the Billing Rule. On hover and focus an item underlines 3px in its own
row's category colour.

#### Illustration slot
Optional, on the intro band's right from 44rem. It carries a marked placeholder until the Owner supplies artwork.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** design the dark theme first and keep the light theme a complete equal, both at WCAG 2.2 AA.
- **Do** open every Page in dark; show light only after the visitor picks it in the switch.
- **Do** make the one primary action per view filled ink, and outline every other button in a hairline.
- **Do** derive every secondary, muted, hairline and tint colour from the ink at its fixed alpha step.
- **Do** lead Home with the facts in a hairline panel (role, seniority, location, work model, technologies) and keep
  the Owner's photo small and plain beside the name.
- **Do** take every visual value from tokens, so About me can restyle the shared navigation, language toggle and Footer
  through tokens alone.
- **Do** give every component all its states (default, hover, focus, active, disabled, and loading, error or success
  where they apply) before it counts as done.
- **Do** keep motion to short expo ease-out transitions (150ms for controls, 200ms for panels, 240ms for the theme
  crossfade), no bounce, and 0ms under reduced motion.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce a hued accent, a gradient or a multi-colour palette; the Owner turned down every one offered.
- **Don't** use error red for anything but errors.
- **Don't** follow the system colour scheme preference; the Owner chose dark always by default.
- **Don't** build a template portfolio: no avatar hero, no skill pills, no grid of identical project cards.
- **Don't** set a monospace eyebrow above a heading, or monospace prose.
- **Don't** make the Owner's photo a centrepiece: no large portrait, no colour effects, no full-bleed photo.
- **Don't** put a shadow on anything that doesn't float above the page.
- **Don't** let expression cost the 30-second read: role, seniority, technologies, location, CV and contact stay
  findable at a glance.

### About me

On About me these replace the Portfolio's colour, theme, photo and type items; taking every value from tokens, giving
every component all its states, the motion and the shadow items hold on both.

#### Do:
- **Do** give every Section one band, rotating purple, sunset, lime and charcoal, with the bar and intro on purple and
  the Footer on the next band in the rotation.
- **Do** let a component read its band's tokens; never set a palette colour on a component.
- **Do** set text in the band's one ink at full strength, and cream only at large sizes on sunset.
- **Do** size the Page title from its container so it runs the band's width, in Big Shoulders Display, uppercase.
- **Do** set lists as billing: the category in large display type in the band's two key colours, the item in heavy
  uppercase Archivo, underlined in its category colour on hover and focus.
- **Do** mark an error with a dashed 3px ink rule and its message.
- **Do** keep the language toggle in its place and leave the theme switch out.
- **Do** make every band work without an illustration; the slot is optional.

#### Don't:
- **Don't** add a shadow, a blur, a gradient or neon; the bands are flat and printed.
- **Don't** step the ink down by alpha; on sunset every step below full ink falls under 4.5:1.
- **Don't** give error a hue; no single hue reads on all four bands.
- **Don't** let it look like a social profile (cards, counters, feeds), a gamer or childish costume, the Portfolio
  recoloured, or anything corporate or cold.
- **Don't** use Dance Gavin Dance's artwork, lettering or logo; the poster grammar is an own version.
- **Don't** add a photo or a second theme.
- **Don't** round a corner unless the thing is truly round.
