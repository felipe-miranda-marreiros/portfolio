# Internationalisation

The Portfolio is bilingual: English (`en`) and Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`), equal in standing ([principle 4](principles.md#4-both-locales-are-equal), [ADR 0004](../../adr/0004-publish-requires-both-locales.md)). The CMS UI is English only, but it edits Content in both Locales.

## Text length

- Design with `pt-BR` first. It runs about 30% longer than `en` on average, and much longer in short labels: "Download CV" becomes "Baixar currículo", "Lately" becomes "Ultimamente".
- No truncation and no ellipsis on Content or UI labels. Text wraps; containers grow.
- No fixed widths or heights on anything that holds text. Buttons, chips, tabs and switches size to their label.
- Every component is checked with its longest real `pt-BR` string, and the gallery shows that state.

## Line breaking

- Display headlines use `text-wrap: balance` or `pretty`; body text uses `pretty`.
- Inline groups that must not split, such as the name with the inline avatar and its comma, are kept together with `white-space: nowrap` on that group only.
- No automatic hyphenation (`hyphens: auto`) in display or headline type. Body text may break long words with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` as a last resort.
- `lang` is always set, so the browser applies the right line-breaking and quotation rules.

## Dates and numbers

- All dates and numbers are formatted with `Intl` (`Intl.DateTimeFormat`, `Intl.NumberFormat`, `Intl.DateTimeFormat.prototype.formatRange`) for the current Locale, never assembled by hand.
- Experience periods show years by default ("2022–2024"); when months are shown they use the Locale's short month ("Mar 2024", "mar. de 2024").
- An ongoing period ends with a translated word ("Present", "Atual"), never an empty dash on its own, because screen readers can't read it as "until now".
- Numerals in dates and lists use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so they line up.

## Fonts

- Every face is self-hosted with subsets covering Latin and Latin Extended-A, which include all `pt-BR` glyphs: `á à â ã é ê í ó ô õ ú ü ç` and their capitals.
- `font-display: swap` with metric-matched fallbacks (`size-adjust`, `ascent-override`), so swapping in the web font causes no layout shift.

## Images

- No text inside images. Anything the Visitor reads is live text, so it can be translated, selected and read aloud.
- Alt text is Content: written by the Owner in both Locales.

## Switching Locale

- The Portfolio's Locale lives in the route (`/en`, `/pt-br`); switching keeps the Visitor on the same page and section.
- The switcher shows short codes (EN, PT), each with an accessible name in its own language ("English", "Português").
- The full switcher pattern is specified in `docs/design/patterns/language-switcher.md` (Patterns step).

## The CMS

- Translatable fields show `en` and `pt-BR` side by side, each labelled with its Locale, and each sized to hold the longer `pt-BR` text without scrolling inside a single-line field.
- A missing Locale is shown as a field state with text ("Required in pt-BR before this can be Published"), not only a colour.
