Action bar

The editor's fixed bar at its foot: the item's state, whether its Draft is saved, what is still missing, and its actions with Publish last. The item's state decides the actions: a new Draft, a Draft over a Published version, and a Published item with nothing new to publish. On both themes. The words are placeholders. The CMS only, so there is no About me version.
