Visual language

Content item row

One Content item in the CMS's Content list: its name over a detail that tells similar items apart, then its State indicator, the whole row leading into the editor. In every state, in a Section and across a 320px phone, on both themes, with placeholder words. The CMS only, so there is no About me version. The Section and the phone respond to the pointer and the keyboard.
