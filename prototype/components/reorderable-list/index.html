Reorderable list

Content items the Owner puts in order by hand, such as the Footer's links: each a Content item row between a drag handle and a pair of move buttons. Default, and the row on hover and focus, on both themes. The first item can't move up and the last can't move down. The words are placeholders. The CMS only, so there is no About me version.
